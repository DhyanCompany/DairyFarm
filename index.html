Traditional Ghee Making - FSMS Certified Process

From Farm to Home with Certified Safety

Every step follows our rigorous Food Safety Management System (FSMS) standards and FSSAI guidelines, ensuring the purity and quality of each product.

Fresh Milk
01

Fresh Milk Reception & Filtration

Our process begins with raw milk reception at our state-of-the-art facility. Every batch undergoes strict quality control following the Food Safety Management System (FSMS) standards. We conduct visual inspection and sensory analysis to ensure the milk meets our premium quality standards. The milk is immediately filtered to remove any impurities, maintaining absolute cleanliness. All equipment is made from food-grade stainless steel (SS-304) and sanitized with food-grade detergents before processing to guarantee zero bacterial contamination.

Quality Control: Temperature monitoring ensures proper chilling (≤ 4°C if stored), creating optimal conditions for the next stage of processing.

02

Heating & Cream Separation

The filtered milk is carefully heated to precise temperatures for pasteurization (63°C for 30 minutes or 72°C for 15 seconds). This critical control point eliminates pathogenic bacteria and ensures the product's safety for consumers. Following pasteurization, we separate the cream through traditional methods or modern centrifugation, depending on the final product. For milk products, the milk is rapidly cooled to ≤ 4°C to slow bacterial growth. For ghee production, the cream is transferred to our ghee kettle for the next transformation stage.

Safety Monitoring: Continuous temperature monitoring using validated thermometers ensures we never fall below critical temperature thresholds. Any deviation triggers immediate corrective actions.

03

Boiling & Clarification for Golden Ghee

The cream is transferred to our traditional large vessel where it undergoes the magical transformation into ghee. The milk solids are boiled at temperatures exceeding 100°C (up to 110°C) in a carefully controlled process. As the moisture evaporates, the clarification process reveals the beautiful golden color and rich aroma that defines authentic desi ghee. This high-temperature boiling is our primary control measure against pathogenic bacteria and mold growth.

The heat evaporates all remaining moisture, creating the shelf-stable product that's been treasured in Indian kitchens for centuries. The process requires careful monitoring of internal temperature, color progression, and aroma development. Our experienced team follows traditional wisdom combined with modern food safety protocols to achieve the perfect clarification point.

04

Filtration, Cooling & Premium Packaging

Once clarification is complete, the golden ghee passes through fine filtration to remove any remaining milk solids and impurities. The filtered ghee undergoes controlled cooling before being transferred to our sterilized Santok containers in our dedicated high-care packing zone. All containers are sealed under hygienic conditions to prevent airborne contamination and maintain the product's purity.

Storage Standards: Our ghee is stored in clean, dry conditions away from direct sunlight at ambient temperature. All packaging maintains integrity with proper sealing verified through inspection. Each jar carries the guarantee of traditional excellence meeting modern food safety certifications. From our farm to your home, every step follows rigorous FSSAI-compliant Food Safety Management System protocols.
